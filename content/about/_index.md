# Giuliano Bortolassi
<div class="alert">
Born in Brazil, around 1983, grew up in a small city in the São Paulo Region. Had his first contact with computers around 1995 (12 years old) as a mean to write video-games. 
With 14 years was already having programming classes (Clipper Summer) and two years after that started to work on a internet provider as a 'support/system admin'. 
<br/><br/>
At that time, together with some friends, launched a local website to publish pictures and had the first contact with the internet business models. At this point had already learned quite a few other programming languages and attended to diverse regional internet conventions. 
<br/><br/>
Later, with 18 years, after serving one year in the local mandatory military service, moved to a bigger city where started to live on the incoming of his programming work, finally becoming a 'professional programmer'.
</div>

### Professional Experience 
From that point, accumulated lots of projects and experience, working for diverse industries. 

* Magazine publisher building the foundations for a intranet framework, a beskpoke internal BI system and automation tools to help to increase the productivity of the authors and designers.

* Software house developing applications for mobile devices (PalmOS handhelds) to improve logistic fleet efficience plus the backend system to support the data sent and received to the devices.

* Spent some years working as a independent consultant, coaching teams on best practices for developing enterprise web applications with 'modern' (for that time) java frameworks. 

* Acted as a technical leader for diverse projects, including the rebuild of a full academinca management system, from inside the university by training the university workforce on modern development stack and implementing agily workflows. 

* After the consultant journey, spent several years working on the financial market. Two of those working for a market data provider, then seven years working on a global investment banking, building quite a few automation tools for backoffice work, implementing a risk calculation tool for the middle desk, refactoring and old monolytich system on a distributed, high scalable one. Lead a projects with internation stackholders and local outsourcing companies to implement KYC validations. In that period learned a lot of ITIL and participaed in both incident response and CM management.

* The next stop was working for the brazillian stock exchange, not as a developer, but as a system/enterprise architect. That's when his system design skills were put to the test as the environment provided plenty of different domains, high scallable systems, different business departments and a variety of constraints.  At this time also learned a lot about presenting projects and selling ideas for C-Level sponsors specially on adoption of cloud infrastructure on a native on premisses culture.

* Looking for new challenges, a quick experience on the biggest bank in Brazil, made him reconsider the Enterprise Architect position and start moving back to the system architect role. After leaving the bank, spend about two years on a smaller, more focused company, providing risk calculation services. In that role, besides helping to design the high available, high throughtput system provided by the company, also was "on call" responding to production incidents and working to improve the resilience in the platform. This was the real 'ownership' experience.

* At some point, around 2021/2022 got invited to join an global payment company, and moved to Europe to work as a Platform Engineer, focused on Observability Tools and pipelines. And that's the current chapter....
